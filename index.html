How to Play

  • ← → or A D — run
  • Space / W / ↑ — flip gravity. Momentum carries through the flip. There is no jump — the flip IS the jump.
  • P pause · M mute · R restart
  • Touch: hold the ◀ ▶ zones to run, tap FLIP to flip.
  • Flip only works while standing on a surface (a short grace window after leaving one).
  • Collect ◈ gold orbs for points. Touch a violet beacon to set a checkpoint.
  • Spikes, saws and crushers are fatal. Death sends you back to your last checkpoint — orbs you collected stay collected.
  • Reach the green exit portal. Beat your best time for a time bonus; finish without dying for a flawless bonus.
